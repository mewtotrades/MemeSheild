Frequently asked

Is this financial advice? ▸
No. MemeShield scores on-chain structure — things like mint authority, freeze authority, and holder concentration — that correlate with past rug pulls. It can't see a creator's intent or private off-chain plans. A low score is not a safety guarantee, and this isn't a recommendation to buy or avoid anything. Always do your own research and never risk more than you can afford to lose.
How accurate is the score? ▸
Solana scans pull real, live on-chain data directly from the blockchain — mint authority, freeze authority, and actual holder concentration. EVM chains (Ethereum, Base, etc.) currently use a lighter heuristic estimate, since deep holder-concentration data there needs a paid indexer we haven't connected yet. We label which type of result you're looking at on every scan.
What happens to my data? ▸
Your account stores only your username, a securely hashed password, your subscription status, and your scan history — enough to make the free-plan limit and paid features work. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe; MemeShield never sees or stores your full card details.

Reach out through the contact link in the footer and we'll take a look at your situation.
Why do I need an account to scan? ▸
Accounts let us enforce the free daily scan limit fairly and let you keep a history of what you've checked. No email or phone number required — just a username, password, and a one-time recovery passkey.
